How to Pay

Four steps. Claude buys, you sign.

  1. 01

    Connect it to Claude

    In Claude, open Plugins, choose Add → Add custom connector, paste the URL below and choose No sign in. In Claude Code, run the command instead. This one URL pays on both Solana and Base: you connect Phantom or Rabby / MetaMask when you approve.

    · · ·
    https://agentpay.x402jp.com/mcp
  2. 02

    Ask Claude to buy

    Paste a paid API’s URL and say “buy this”, or let Claude find one. AgentPay checks the seller, the price, your limits and whether the payment can go through. If a check fails, Claude tells you why and nothing is prepared.

  3. 03

    Open the link and sign

    Claude shows an approval link with the price, the payee, the API and the network. Sign in Phantom on Solana, or Rabby / MetaMask on Base. On a phone, open it in the Phantom app.

  4. 04

    Tell Claude “approved”

    Claude fetches the result: the API response, the transaction found on chain and the delivery check. A link that isn’t approved expires in 5 minutes.

Before and after every payment

Checks you would forget to run yourself.

Seller record

Looks the seller up in Seller Probe. If its latest paid purchase was paid but not delivered, the payment is stopped.

Price and input

Compares the price the API asks with its catalog listing, and checks that the request carries the input the API requires.

Spending limits

At most $0.10 per payment and $1.00 per wallet per 24 hours. You can lower them in your connector URL. Claude can’t change them.

Payment setup

Skips a seller whose payment service rejects wallet-signed payments, or whose address can’t receive USDC, and pays on its other network when it can.

Your wallet

Stops before you sign if your wallet is set up as a smart account (an EIP-7702 delegation) that this kind of payment can’t use, and says how to fix it.

Built when you sign

The payment is checked again and built only after you press sign, so it never goes stale. The amount you sign must match the amount you saw.

Confirmed on chain

After paying, AgentPay finds the transfer on chain itself instead of trusting the seller’s reply. The same payment is never sent twice.

Recovers non-delivery

If a seller takes the payment but doesn’t deliver, and it publishes Delivery Receipt keys, AgentPay fetches the response again with the same payment.

Explains every stop

When AgentPay stops or a seller refuses, Claude gets what happened, whose side the problem is on and what to do next, and nothing is paid.
